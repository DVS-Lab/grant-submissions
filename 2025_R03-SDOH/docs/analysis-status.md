# SDOH analysis status

## Automated in the reproducible working pipeline

- The private authoritative workbook contains 709 unique retained participants;
  attention-check and `gc` validation introduce no new exclusion.
- OAFEM uses a public-safe, unique 30-item stem map and published 1/2/3 severity
  hierarchy. The primary result is the weighted sum of rated 0/1/2 items;
  unweighted complete-case scoring is diagnostic only.
- PROMIS-29 Profile v2.0 has correct item directions and official
  HealthMeasures T-score conversion for seven domains, plus 0–10 pain intensity.
- The 24-item Convex Time Budget task is recovered from exact choice strings;
  every observed allocation maps to 1–6, with a total mean and four descriptive
  delay-pair subscales.
- Fraud fields are explicitly lifetime loss and past-12-month loss, each with an
  ordinal band and binary any-loss indicator.
- Blank, whitespace-only, `NA`, and `N/A` strings are missing before factors or
  models are built.
- The current-ZIP backbone links reproducibly to 2022 UDS ZCTA, Robert Graham
  Center SDI, 2012–2022 ACAG PM2.5, Opportunity Insights Social Capital Atlas,
  ACS 2024 five-year B19083 Gini, and USDA 2020 RUCA. ZIP/ZCTA are excluded from
  the 709-row private analysis master.
- Exposures retain raw and standardized forms; a secondary equal-weight burden
  composite requires at least three of four fixed, documented adversity domains.
- The private exposure-correlation audit flags |r| ≥ .70 and ≥ .85.
- All 216 fixed Family A–G model specifications run, including raw/standardized
  effects, logistic/ordinal fraud models, OAFEM sensitivities, centered
  interactions, and long-residence comparisons.
- Seventeen of 35 historical formulas now run. The remaining 18 require the
  unrecoverable old `minority` recode, which is not fabricated.
- Eleven private figures and a balanced private results/review packet are
  regenerated by the one-command pipeline.

## Deliberately unavailable

- No ZIP variable called ADI is generated. Neighborhood Atlas validates ADI at
  block-group geography, and a defensible ZCTA sensitivity would require an
  approved credentialed vintage plus documented population weights.
- No national ZIP crime/disorder score is generated. The source audit found no
  consistent contemporary national ZIP/ZCTA product; coarser FBI/UCR or
  tract-level academic alternatives require a substantive tradeoff decision.
