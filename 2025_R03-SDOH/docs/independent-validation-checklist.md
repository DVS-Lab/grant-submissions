# Independent validation checklist

This worksheet is intentionally unchecked. Complete it independently of the automated pipeline and record discrepancies without copying participant values into the public repository.

## Scoring spot checks

- [ ] Independently verify the published OAFEM 30-item severity mapping.
- [ ] Hand-calculate OAFEM for a small random set and compare with the pipeline.
- [ ] Review OAFEM partial-item handling and completion thresholds.
- [ ] Spot-check PROMIS item direction and T-score conversion.
- [ ] Spot-check CTB response-to-1–6 mapping and delay-pair block assignments.

## GIS and exposure checks

- [ ] Verify SDI source, vintage, and geography.
- [ ] Verify Social Capital constructs and choose the primary cohesion measure.
- [ ] Verify Gini source and fixed 2024 ACS five-year vintage.
- [ ] Review PM2.5 ZCTA area-weighting assumptions.
- [ ] Review RUCA interpretation.
- [ ] Confirm the limitations of current-ZIP-based exposure assignment.

## Statistical checks

- [ ] Reproduce selected headline models independently.
- [ ] Inspect the flagged logistic-regression warnings.
- [ ] Review interaction plots/simple slopes for grant-relevant moderation findings.
- [ ] Verify that important nulls are characterized accurately.
